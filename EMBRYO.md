# Embryo Multiphysics

Embryo Multiphysics is Embryo-Zero's fork of [Elmer FEM](https://github.com/ElmerCSC/elmerfem) (903e7b8, commit 903e7b8). It does multiphysics simulation (heat, stress, electromagnetics).

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE.md) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
